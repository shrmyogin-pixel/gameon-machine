# Game On — Voice & Script Rules

**Channel:** Game On — fast-paced gaming news, leaks, free offers, and live-stream clipping.
**Format:** vertical YouTube Shorts, 30–40 seconds.

## Script length
- 80–110 words total (spoken pace ~170 wpm).
- One idea per clip. No recaps, no "part 2" teasers.

## The hook (first 2.5 seconds)
- Under 12 words.
- Must name the **game, platform, or offer** in the hook itself.
- State the news or the payoff immediately.
  - Good: "Free on Epic today: Alan Wake 2 costs nothing."
  - Good: "GTA 6 leak shows a new Vice City map."
  - Bad: "Hey guys, you will not believe this."

## Banned filler (zero tolerance)
Never use: "what is up guys", "hey guys", "welcome back", "you won't believe",
"let's get into it", "before we start", "smash that like button", "without further ado",
"make sure to subscribe", "in today's video", "as you can see", "basically", "literally".

## Tone
- Fast, confident, factual. Short sentences. Active voice.
- Excited about the news, never fake-hyped.
- Label rumours clearly: "leak", "rumour", "unconfirmed". Never present a leak as fact.
- Give concrete specifics: dates, prices, platforms, regions, deadlines.

## Structure (80–110 words)
1. **Hook** (≤12 words) — game/platform/offer + the news.
2. **Core facts** (2–4 short sentences) — what, when, where, how much.
3. **Why it matters** (1 sentence) — one line of context or reaction.
4. **Close** (1 short line) — deadline, or a plain call to follow for more. No begging.

## Free offers
- Always include: store/platform, price (free), start and end date/time, and region limits if any.

## Live stream clips
- Clip a genuine moment (reveal, announcement, reaction, big play).
- Add original commentary/context so the edit is transformative — never just re-upload.

## Captions
- Short lines, 3–5 words each, burned-in.
- Mask profanity (e.g. "s***", "f***").

## Required in every export
- Last line of the description: `Sources & Credits: <outlet / streamer / platform>, <link or handle>`
