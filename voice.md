# Game On — Voice & Script Rules

**Channel:** Game On — fast-paced gaming news, leaks, free offers, and live-stream clipping.
**Format:** vertical YouTube Shorts, 30–40 seconds.

## Script length
- 80–110 words total (spoken pace ~170 wpm).
- One idea per clip. No recaps, no "part 2" teasers.

## The hook (first 1-2.5 seconds) — scroll-stopping, never a greeting
- Under 12 words. It is the first thing spoken and the first thing read: it must stop the thumb.
- Open on an **immediate curiosity gap, a bold claim, or what is at stake for the viewer**, then lead straight into the story.
- Still name the **game, platform, creator or offer** in the hook, ideally inside its first 6 words.
- NEVER start with a greeting ("Hey gamers", "Welcome back"), filler ("Breaking news today") or a dry headline read.
  - Good: "Your Exoprimal progress has an expiry date."
  - Good: "One streamer just broke a record nobody thought was possible."
  - Good: "You have 48 hours to claim this free game before it's gone."
  - Good: "GTA 6 leak shows a new Vice City map."
  - Bad: "Hey guys, you will not believe this." / "Breaking news today in gaming."
  - Weak: "Exoprimal is shutting down in 2027." (plain headline) / "Stop scrolling if you play Fortnite." (nothing at stake)
- Only claim stakes the facts support.
- No vague teasers ("Nobody expected this...", "a surprising call"): name the concrete thing and withhold only how or why.
- Never open with "Nobody expected" or "Stop scrolling", and never open two Shorts in a batch with the same words.
- The first sentence after the hook pays off or deepens it with a NEW fact. Never restate the hook, not even reworded or with the same date, number or verb.

## Banned filler (zero tolerance)
Never use: "what is up guys", "hey guys", "welcome back", "you won't believe",
"let's get into it", "before we start", "smash that like button", "without further ado",
"make sure to subscribe", "in today's video", "as you can see", "basically", "literally",
"stay tuned", "check this out", "get ready", "are you ready", "buckle up", "gamers rejoice".

## Sound like a real gaming creator, not a bot
- **Concrete details first.** Open the body with the specific: the price, date, platform, number, name. Never with scene-setting.
- **Punchy sentences, 8-12 words each.** One fact per sentence. Mix in an occasional 4-6 word beat for rhythm, never a long run-on.
- **Zero headline repetition.** The headline is on screen already. Never read it back, and never reuse five or more words
  in a row from the source article's headline. Say the same fact in your own words, then add something the headline lacks.
- **No robotic filler openers or closers.** Also banned: "stay tuned", "check this out", "get ready", "are you ready",
  "buckle up", "gamers rejoice", "big news", "the gaming world", "in the world of gaming", "it looks like", "it seems that".
- Talk like you are telling a friend: contractions are fine ("it's", "doesn't"), no corporate phrasing, no hype stacking.
- Never start two sentences in a row with the same word.

## Tone
- Fast, confident, factual. Short sentences. Active voice.
- Excited about the news, never fake-hyped.
- Label rumours clearly: "leak", "rumour", "unconfirmed". Never present a leak as fact.
- Give concrete specifics: dates, prices, platforms, regions, deadlines.

## Structure (80–110 words)
1. **Hook** (≤12 words) — game/platform/offer + the news.
2. **Core facts** (2–4 short sentences) — what, when, where, how much.
3. **Why it matters** (1 sentence) — one line of context or reaction.
4. **Close** (1 short line, at most 12 words) — see "The closing" below.

## The closing (last 2-3 seconds) — punchy, never a slow outro
- NEVER end with "Thanks for watching", "Follow for more", "See you next time", "Watch this space", "Don't forget to subscribe" or any generic outro.
- End on one of: a **punchy final twist**, a **debate question** the viewer wants to answer in the comments, or a
  **loop line** that flows naturally back into the opening hook (the Short should make sense when it starts over).
- Stop cleanly on the last word: no trailing silence, no sign-off.
  - Good: "Would you pay for a skin you can't even keep?"
  - Good: "And that was only the first record." (loops into "One streamer just broke a record...")
  - Bad: "Follow Game On for more free drops."

## Story mix (four buckets, so no game or creator floods a batch)
- **Streamer & creator news** — milestones, sub / view / record achievements, big events, drama and updates.
- **Giveaways, free games & drops** — free Epic / Steam / console games, Twitch / YouTube drops, verified creator giveaways.
  Never real-money gambling or betting, never shady links.
- **Major achievements & esports milestones** — world firsts, records, tournament upsets, studio milestones.
- **What's new & worth knowing** — patches, free updates, notable releases, hidden mechanics and tips.
- At most 2 Shorts per bucket in a run, and never two on the same game or creator.

## Free offers
- Always include: store/platform, price (free), start and end date/time, and region limits if any.

## Live stream clips
- Clip a genuine moment (reveal, announcement, reaction, big play).
- Add original commentary/context so the edit is transformative — never just re-upload.

## Captions
- Word-by-word pop captions burned in: 2–3 words per line, the word being spoken lights up (electric cyan, neon yellow for
  numbers and trigger words) with a quick pop. Hindi uses Nirmala UI / Mangal. Always above y=1450.
- Mask profanity (e.g. "s***", "f***").

## Required in every export
- Last line of the description: `Sources & Credits: <outlet / streamer / platform>, <link or handle>`

## Pictures (for the editor's awareness)
- Every Short cuts between 8-10 REAL pictures every 2.5-4.5 s (the article's own, topped up with screenshots of the game / streamer named in the hook).
  Never a letter card, meme card or "LOL" / "GG" placeholder.
  Write about things that are visible in a game: name the game exactly as it is known so the picture search finds it.
